────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. 🏆 BEST OVERALL: Silhouette Method (Exp 1)
   • Best orthogonality (+0.362)
   • Good quality (82.24%), fast training
   • Clean 3-category structure
   • Simple, proven, stable

2. ❌ HYBRID FAILED: Gradient Refinement Counterproductive
   • WORSENED orthogonality: +0.362 → +0.562 (55% degradation!)
   • Small quality gain (+1.46pp) not worth orthogonality cost
   • 500 epochs gradient optimization made things worse
   • More complex, slower than clustering-only
   • ⚠️ Based on limited sample (~2-5K), but trend is clear

3. BEST RECONSTRUCTION: Elbow Method (but don't use it)
   • Highest quality (84.45%) but at severe cost
   • 12 categories = over-clustering (many tiny clusters)
   • Second-worst orthogonality (+0.809)
   • Complex, unstable, slow training

4. WORST PERFORMER: Gap Statistic
   • Poor quality (73.48%), worst reconstruction
   • Bad orthogonality (+0.636)
   • Over-clusters (5 categories vs silhouette's 3)
   • No advantages over silhouette

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CRITICAL TRADE-OFFS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

RECONSTRUCTION vs ORTHOGONALITY:

                High Orthogonality (low correlation)
                       ↑
                       |
         Silhouette • |
                     | |
                   Avg | •
                       |   • Elbow
    ──────────────────┼──────────────────────→
                       |           High Reconstruction
                       |
                  Gap  • (poor on both)

Clear trade-off: Cannot optimize both simultaneously with clustering alone.
  → Fewer clusters = Better orthogonality, worse reconstruction
  → More clusters = Better reconstruction, worse orthogonality

SOLUTION: Hybrid method (clustering + gradient refinement) attempts to
          break this trade-off by optimizing orthogonality directly

FINAL RECOMMENDATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION:

  ✅ Use SILHOUETTE CLUSTERING-ONLY (Exp 1)

     Config:
       method: "clustering"  # NOT hybrid
       k_selection_method: "silhouette"
       linkage_method: "ward"

     Why:
       • BEST orthogonality: +0.362 (vs hybrid's +0.562)
       • Good quality: 82.24% (only -1.46pp behind hybrid)
       • Simple pipeline: No gradient refinement complexity
       • Faster training: 115s vs hybrid's 153s
       • Proven stable: No risk of degradation
       • Clean 3-category structure

     Trade-off: Accept 1.46pp lower quality for better orthogonality

SECONDARY ALTERNATIVES:

  For maximum reconstruction quality (if orthogonality less critical):
    → Average Linkage (Exp 2)
      • 83.37% quality
      • Must verify orthogonality first (currently unknown)
      Config: method=clustering, k_selection=gap_statistic, linkage=average

  If willing to sacrifice orthogonality for 2pp quality gain:
    → Hybrid Method (Exp 4)
      • 83.70% quality, best val loss (0.577)
      • But worse orthogonality (+0.562 vs +0.362)
      • NOT RECOMMENDED based on primary objective
